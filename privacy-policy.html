FitFlow Privacy Policy

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name and email address (Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile and plan inputs: fitness level, main goal, days per week, session length, and body areas you ask us to go easy on (knees, shoulders, lower back, wrists).
  • Activity data: workouts completed, minutes, streaks and badges.
  • Nutrition data: meals you log, portion size, calories and macros, and meal photos you scan or choose.
  • Community data: challenges you join, your progress in them, and your sharing setting (Only me, Private circle, Everyone).
  • Technical data: crash reports and basic diagnostics.

2. How we use it

To create and adjust your workout plan, show your progress, estimate nutrition, run challenges and keep the app working. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. AI processing

Daily workout plans are generated on your device from your answers. Meal photos are analysed to estimate the food and nutrition. AI output is a suggestion and can be wrong; it is not medical advice.

4. Social features and visibility

You control who sees your finished workouts: Only me, Private circle (the default) or Everyone. You can change this at any time in the Community screen. Circle members only see activity you have chosen to share.

5. Storage and service providers

Data is stored with Google Firebase (authentication and database) as our service provider and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data may be processed outside your country under standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Our legal basis is contract (running your account) and consent (optional features such as camera and sharing). You may access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. Use Delete account in the app or email fitflow.support@gmail.com. We delete your data within 30 days of an account-deletion request.

7. Health data and HIPAA

FitFlow is a wellness app, not a healthcare provider or a HIPAA covered entity, so HIPAA does not apply. We still treat fitness and nutrition data as sensitive and share it only as described here.

8. Permissions

Camera / photo library: only to scan or choose a meal photo. Notifications: only for workout and meal reminders you enable.

9. Children

FitFlow is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and tell you in the app for material changes.

11. Medical disclaimer

FitFlow gives general fitness and nutrition information. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.
